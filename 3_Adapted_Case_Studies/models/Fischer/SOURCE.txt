Fischer mutual exclusion

Source:
The official UPPAAL Fischer benchmark generator by M. O. Moller, available
from the UPPAAL benchmark collection, with k=2.

Adaptation:
This is the two-process instance. The finite shared identifier is represented
by an explicit component, while identifier tests and assignments are exposed
as indexed synchronization actions. The location invariant is transferred to
the corresponding outgoing guard. Additional resets are placed only where the
old clock value is not used again.
