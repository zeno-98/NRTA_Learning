Bridge and torch

Source:
The bridge.xml demonstration model distributed with UPPAAL.

Adaptation:
The packaged benchmark is the three-participant instance with crossing times
5, 10, and 20 minutes represented uniformly as 1, 2, and 4 five-minute units.
The torch component records its side and holder count; participant identity is
already carried by the synchronized participant locations. Indexed take and
release actions make each handshake explicit.
